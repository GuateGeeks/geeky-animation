Things to try

  • Drag Geeky around (pop it out first) and fling it. The frames swing, stretch and land with a squash.
  • Tap to poke, double-tap for a random animation, or long-press for the menu. A mouse works too.
  • Shake your cursor around Geeky. Or click it five times fast.
  • Type “Dear Geeky,” in GeekyWriter. You know what happens.
  • Leave it alone for half a minute: it gets bored, then falls asleep. Move to wake it.
  • Hover , copy some text, scroll to the bottom, or enter ↑↑↓↓←→←→BA.
  • In 3-D, drag Geeky on the stage to spin it by hand. It springs back to face you.
